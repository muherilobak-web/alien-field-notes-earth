# Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review

A one-line review of planet Earth written by an alien tourist, in the voice of a genuine travel review: an enthusiastic five-star rating built around one specific Earth food and the experience of enjoying it, closing with a recommendation for fellow visitors. The entry is a single line of English text, at most 200 characters, produced by a dependency-free Node program that writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
